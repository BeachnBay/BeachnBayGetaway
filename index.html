THE GETAWAY

The Best of Beach & Bay

Enjoy the best of St. Pete Beach from your own private waterfront retreat. Relax by the heated pool and spa, spend the day on the bay, or leave the car behind and walk to the beach, restaurants, bars, recreation and entertainment.

WATERFRONT LIVING

Your Backyard Is the Bay

Start the morning with sunrise over the water, relax in the heated pool and spa, or head straight onto the bay. The private dock includes a boat lift and two jet ski lifts, with paddleboards and a kayak ready for exploring. Fish right from the dock — and keep an eye out for dolphins passing by.

SPACE + PRIVACY

Room to Spread Out

The main home features three bedrooms and two beautifully remodeled baths, an open-concept kitchen and expansive glass overlooking the pool and bay.

A separate upstairs one-bedroom apartment has its own private entrance, full kitchen, living area and patio — a great setup for sharing the getaway with another family while still having your own space.

ONE OF THE BEST PARTS

Leave the Car Parked

Walk to Upham Beach, restaurants, bars, the local brewery and distillery, parks, the St. Pete Beach Recreation Center, gym, classes and frequent community events and concerts. Enjoy the beach-town lifestyle without having to drive everywhere.
